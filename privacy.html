Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026

This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and it does not collect personal information from visitors.

Stored on your device

The site does not use cookies. It saves one setting in your browser's local storage, your choice of High FX or Low FX, so the page looks the same on your next visit. The setting is never sent anywhere, and clearing your browsing data removes it.

Third parties

Fonts, icons, scripts and images are all served from this site, so loading a page sends no requests to other companies. The exception is YouTube: opening a project that includes a video loads the player from youtube-nocookie.com. Google then receives your IP address and can store data in your browser once the video plays, under the Google Privacy Policy. A content blocker can stop the player from loading.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's servers log basic request data, such as your IP address and browser type, for security. I cannot see those logs. The GitHub Privacy Statement explains how GitHub handles them.

Email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply. I never share them or add anyone to a mailing list, and I will delete our emails if you ask.

Other sites

Links to GitHub, Google Drive and YouTube lead to sites with their own privacy policies. On a computer, the Email Me button opens Gmail, which is covered by Google's.

Your rights

Since I hold no data about visitors, there is nothing to access or delete under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, apart from any email you have sent me. The site is not directed at children.

Changes

When this policy changes, so does the date above. Every revision is public in the site's repository.
